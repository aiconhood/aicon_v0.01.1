Three steps, one swap

From wallet to best price, with nothing sitting in between.

01

Connect your wallet

Link any wallet that speaks JSON-RPC. AICON prompts Robinhood Chain automatically if it's not added yet.

02

Get the best route

AICON pulls live quotes from Uniswap, Fables, and other pools on the chain, and picks the one that pays you the most.

03

Swap, non-custodially

Your tokens go straight from your wallet to the chosen pool and back. Either the swap lands at your price, or it reverts.

Built for a chain full of tokenized stocks

Robinhood Chain isn't just another EVM chain. The routing has to account for that.

Multi-DEX routing

Checks Uniswap's AMM, Fables' dynamic-fee pools, and other venues live on Robinhood Chain, then routes to whichever gives the best output right now.

Non-custodial by design

AICON never holds your funds between steps. There's no in-between state where a swap can get stuck.

Aware of stock-token behavior

Stock tokens trade on a 24/7 chain against a market that isn't. Spreads move with market hours even when the chain never sleeps, and routing accounts for that instead of treating every pair the same.

No token, no catch

Nothing to buy first. AICON is a tool, not a launch.
